Some Information about me.

My name is Mario Espinoza-Gutierrez, and I’ve been involved in web development for several years. What began as a simple hobby quickly grew into something I dedicate myself to daily with genuine enthusiasm. I’m especially passionate about building modern, visually appealing web applications, and I enjoy using Next.js and React because it allows me to work with the latest approaches in web development. Even though I haven’t worked professionally in this field yet, I’m motivated by curiosity, creativity, and the desire to learn quickly and efficiently.

I chose IU Indianapolis because it offers an environment where I can grow academically while also exploring my interests in technology. The campus community, opportunities for hands‑on learning, and the support for student innovation made it feel like the right place for me to continue developing both personally and professionally.

To me, being an Honors student means setting clear goals for my future and actively seeking experiences that help me grow. It means challenging myself, staying committed to learning, and pushing beyond my comfort zone. The Honors College encourages me to think critically, communicate effectively, and engage with diverse perspectives—values that align with the kind of student and developer I want to become.

My academic and career goals include becoming a skilled full‑stack developer and contributing to projects that make the online world more modern, accessible, and engaging. Personally, I hope to continue improving my problem‑solving abilities, collaborate with others who share my passion, and build a portfolio that reflects my growth. Outside of academics, I enjoy playing badminton and rewatching episodes of The Walking Dead, which gives me a fun break from coding.

Through the Honors experience, I hope to gain stronger leadership skills, deeper connections with peers and mentors, and opportunities that push me to refine both my technical and personal strengths. Ultimately, I want to build a future where every website feels modern, meaningful, and thoughtfully designed—and where I continue learning with purpose.
